Comprehensive Architecture

Intelligent Fault Protection Framework

High-speed data acquisition combined with machine learning models to detect, classify, and isolate grid faults before cascade blackouts happen.

Transient Fault Classification

Instantaneous discrimination between Line-to-Ground (L-G), Line-to-Line (L-L), and Double Line-to-Ground (L-L-G) faults using high-frequency waveform analysis.

  • Asymmetrical fault detection
  • Symmetrical 3-phase short circuits
  • Wavelet transform filtering

Substation Distance Localization

Pinpoints fault location down to within ±100 meters of overhead transmission lines, drastically cutting field maintenance downtime and patrol costs.

  • Traveling wave impedance calculation
  • Precise tower coordinates logging
  • GPS synchronized timestamping

  • Transformer oil temperature telemetry
  • Harmonic distortion measurement
  • Dynamic thermal line rating (DTLR)
